How to play

  1. 1Place one stone on any empty square or push a line. Never both in the same turn.
  2. 2To push, select your stone at the end of an uninterrupted row or column. The space beyond the other end must be empty. All stones in that line shift one square, even opponents' stones.
  3. 3No immediate reversal: stones that were pushed cannot be pushed in the opposite direction until the player who pushed them finishes their next turn. Perpendicular pushes are still allowed.
  4. 4Make 5 of your own stones in a straight line—horizontal, vertical, or diagonal—to win at the end of your turn.

Install LINE SHIFT

Publish the included files to an HTTPS website, then open the link in Safari on your iPhone.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (you might first tap the page menu).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.

LINE SHIFT opens like an app from your Home Screen, works offline after its first successful load, and saves games on your device. Multiplayer is on one shared device, not online.
